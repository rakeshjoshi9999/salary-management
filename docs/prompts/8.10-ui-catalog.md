## Layout

The list sits in one rounded card: a toolbar (search with an icon, the three filters, and on phones the
Filters and Sort controls), a chip row for the active filters, then the table (cards on phones) and the
paginator. Add employee moves into the page header. Rows and cards get initials avatars; the row of the
employee open in the drawer is highlighted. Behaviour (URL state, debounce, sort whitelist, page sizes)
is unchanged.
