## Outcome

`ListQueryPlanReportTest` (tagged `performance`, outside CI) explains the exact SQL the app builds over
10,000 synthetic rows; results are in `docs/PERFORMANCE.md`. Every list shape runs in under 4 ms on the
server. Decisions from the report: the sort indexes stay single-column for now, with a re-check on
seeded stage (6.4) because the synthetic data is nearly tie-free and the deep page spends about 1.4 ms
on the `id` tiebreak sort; no index is dropped.

Finding: the V1 trigram indexes are skipped until VACUUM, because rows inserted into an existing GIN
index wait in its pending list. Confirmed on local Postgres with `pgstattuple` (10,000 pending tuples,
0 after VACUUM); after `VACUUM ANALYZE` search uses a BitmapOr of both trigram indexes (0.1 ms instead of
8 ms). Task 6.3 now ends the seed with `VACUUM ANALYZE`, outside the seed transaction.
