## Outcome

Neon provisioned through the Vercel marketplace — Postgres 18, Singapore.

**The engineer's route was better than the one Claude documented.** Because the
integration stores the connection details as separate `PG*` variables rather than only a
URL, `DB_URL` could be assembled from a host and database name with no scheme rewriting
or credential stripping — removing the step Claude had called the fiddliest in the whole
sequence.

Claude's contribution here was mostly the warnings: take the pooled endpoint, not the
unpooled one; `SM_POSTGRES_URL_NO_SSL` must not be used because Neon requires TLS; and
Render's region must match Neon's or every query crosses an ocean.

It also noticed the integration had quietly enabled **Neon Auth**, which
`REQUIREMENTS.md` puts out of scope — unused surface, flagged rather than touched.

Postgres 18 then forced a parity change: the test container and local compose were
repinned from 17 to 18, which surfaced a genuine breaking change in the official image
(18+ stores data in a major-version subdirectory and expects the volume one level up).
