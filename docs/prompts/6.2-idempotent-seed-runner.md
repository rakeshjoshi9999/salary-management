## Outcome

`EmployeeSeeder` skips seed employees whose email is already active and backs that up with
`ON CONFLICT (lower(email)) WHERE deleted_at IS NULL DO NOTHING`. It inserts everything in one
transaction, so a failure leaves no rows. A rerun inserts nothing and takes no ids from the sequence.
`SeedRunner` runs only under the `seed` profile:
`cd backend && ./mvnw spring-boot:run -Dspring-boot.run.profiles=seed`.
