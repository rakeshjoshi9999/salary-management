## Outcome

Schema designed and recorded in [`DATA_MODEL.md`](../DATA_MODEL.md) and ADR-008 to ADR-012. No code was
written; the migration is task 3.1.

The engineer overrode the AI's recommendation in five places:
- **Titles depend on departments** (AI had recommended independent lists). After the pushback, the AI
  agreed: with natural-key FKs, the extra cost is one composite FK.
- **Soft delete** instead of hard delete.
- **Sort indexes created now**, not deferred until measured in 4.5.
- **A 1950-01-01 lower bound on hire date**, a rule beyond `REQUIREMENTS.md`.
- **Five countries** instead of the eight proposed.

Switching to one question at a time, at the engineer's request, surfaced follow-ups the batched list
had missed: whether `employee` should still store department once the title implies it, email reuse
after soft delete, and a restore feature.
