## Outcome

[`UI_DESIGN.md`](../UI_DESIGN.md) holds the routes, desktop and phone wireframes, five Mermaid flows, a
state table and formatting rules. The decisions are in ADR-027 to ADR-029.

The engineer overrode the AI in several places:
- **Insights as the landing page**, not the list.
- **"Catalog"** as the section name, at `/catalog`.
- **A backend Settings feature** for the default country (India), not `localStorage`. This is new scope:
  task 5.6, task 7.8, and additions to `openapi.yaml` and `DATA_MODEL.md`.
- **Fully responsive**, phone included.
- **Real charts**: `chart.js` approved as a new dependency, for a doughnut of department headcount
  (the AI had recommended a horizontal bar).

The AI decided some smaller details without asking. All are listed in ADR-027 for review: a
"Discard changes?" prompt, tablet columns, the unknown-country fallback, and the 300 ms debounce.
